Role icons in UMBRA

Infanterie, Medic, Builders, Driver and Pilote reproduce the shapes shown in
the Wardogs class screenshot supplied by the team. They are vector redraws,
not extracted original game files. Wardogs is a BULKHEAD game.
Launcher is an UMBRA rocket-launcher silhouette.
Recon, Sniper and Libre use Lucide icons under the included Lucide license.
